Pull Out Roppongi Hills: Tokyo

Short description (80 characters max)
Swipe up to yank skyscrapers out of a real 3D Tokyo, with Tokyo Tower & Mt Fuji

Full description
Roppongi Hills towers are sprouting all over Tokyo's Minato City like bamboo shoots!
Swipe up to pull them out, roots and all. THUD — down they go in a cloud of dust.
Catch them early for an easy pull and big points. Leave them and they take root, until the whole city is covered in towers...!

■ The real Tokyo in 3D
・About 100,000 buildings and the road network rebuilt from PLATEAU, Japan's open 3D city models
・A 5 km stage around Roppongi: Azabu, Akasaka, Toranomon, Shimbashi, Shiodome, Shinagawa, Shibuya and Shinjuku
・See Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, the Rainbow Bridge, and Mt. Fuji on the horizon
・Towers even sprout in the green of the Imperial Palace and Meiji Shrine

■ Simple swipe controls
・Swipe up near a tower to yank it out of the ground
・A "Morning-fresh" one that has just peeked out is worth 500 points. The bigger it grows, the longer the swipe
・Pulled towers fly through the air and crash down sideways in a cloud of dust
・Even rooted towers come out with a few more swipes

■ Pull up a subway train too
・Towers that sprout near a subway line have a train tangled in their roots
・Pull it up with the train for a bonus!

■ Hunt across the city
・Swipe left or right to turn, swipe down with two fingers to move forward, pinch to zoom
・Edge arrows and the radar show where new towers are sprouting. Tap to fly there
・If 5 towers take root at once, Minato City falls. You have 90 seconds

■ And more
・Ranks from "Apprentice Digger" to "Guardian of Minato"
・Share your score and rank with friends
・Combo bonuses for pulling towers in a row
・When the city falls, watch an ad to keep playing (once per game)
・Original festival-style music and sound effects
・Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
・English and Japanese

A quick, satisfying way to kill time in the streets of Tokyo. How many can you pull out?

This is an unofficial game and is not affiliated with Mori Building Co., Ltd. or any real facility or transit operator.
Buildings and roads: based on the 3D city models of Project PLATEAU by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism of Japan (MLIT).
